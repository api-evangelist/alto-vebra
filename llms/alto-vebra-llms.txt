# Alto

> Alto is the UK's most widely used cloud-based CRM for estate and letting agents, used by more than 6,000 agency branches. It brings sales, lettings, marketing, client accounting, compliance and reporting into one platform, with AI and automation built in. Alto is part of Houseful. Pricing is quote-based — book a demo for a tailored quote.

Alto helps estate and letting agencies win more instructions, process tenancies faster, stay compliant, and cut admin time. Independent agents and multi-branch networks use it to run sales and lettings end to end.

## What Alto does
- [Sales CRM](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/sales-crm): pipeline, lead tracking and sales progression for estate agents
- [Lettings CRM](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/lettings-crm): end-to-end lettings management and tenancy processing
- [Lettings progression](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/lettings-progression): move-in and tenancy setup automation
- [Sales progression software](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/property-sales-progression-for-estate-agents): manage sales from offer to completion
- [Client accounting software](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/client-accounting-software): lettings and client money accounting
- [Property marketing](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/property-marketing-software): portals, brochures and listings
- [Multi-branch](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/multi-branch) and [single-branch](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/single-branch) setups
- [Our integrations](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/integrations): connected portals, tools and services
- [Product features](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/product-features): full feature overview
